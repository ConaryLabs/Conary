# Conary Code of Conduct

Conary adopts the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/)
for all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, Discussions, review
channels, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

We expect participants to be respectful, constructive, and mindful of the
safety and dignity of others. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
intimidation, publication of another person's private information, and other
conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, moderate project
spaces, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when needed to enforce this policy. Reports will be evaluated in context and
handled as privately as reasonably possible.

Report conduct concerns privately to `peter@conary.io`. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive personal information. For software security
vulnerabilities, use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy incorporates the Contributor Covenant 3.0 by reference. The
Contributor Covenant is stewarded by the Organization for Ethical Source and
